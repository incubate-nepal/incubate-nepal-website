    2025 - Policy Making

    Team bureaucrazy.co

    Mentor: Aakriti Ghimire
    Peer-Mentor: Preeti Pantha
    Students: Akrishta Dahal, Rishavi Gautam, Sudipti Mainali, Drishya Puri, Manish Singh Mahato, Sanskritii Baniya


    Team bureaucrazy.co is a project under Incubate Nepal that aims to create a platform for Nepali youth to engage with the government and policy-making process. The team will work on various projects, including research, advocacy, and community engagement. The goal of the project is to empower Nepali youth to become active participants in the democratic process and to create a more inclusive and transparent government.
